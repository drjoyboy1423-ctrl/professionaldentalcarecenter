Privacy Policy

ProDental Records app · Last updated: 2 November 2026

ProDental Records is a management app used by dental clinics (for example KAL Speciality Dental Clinic and Bilal Speciality Dental Clinic) to register patients, book appointments, keep dental records, issue invoices and manage staff. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is protected.

1. Who uses the app and who owns the data

The app is for clinic staff only (administrator, manager, doctor, nurse and reception). Patients do not create accounts or sign in. Each clinic is responsible for the patient information it enters. The app operator acts as the service provider that stores that information for the clinic.

2. Information the app handles

  • Staff accounts: name, username, email, phone, role, professional details, profile photo and sign-in credentials.
  • Patient records entered by the clinic: name, phone, age, sex, date of birth, card number, photo, dental and medical history, diagnoses, treatments, prescriptions, tooth photos and X-ray images, lab and X-ray orders, appointments, invoices, payments and refunds.
  • Clinic operations: dental material stock, purchase requests, sterilization test logs, expenses and salary records (visible only to managers and the administrator).
  • Technical data: an activity log of who changed what and when, and notification settings kept on your device.

The app does not use advertising, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or rent any information.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera / photos: only when you choose to take or upload a patient photo, receipt photo, tooth photo, or scan a patient barcode. Images are saved to the patient record, not elsewhere.
  • Notifications: optional alerts for new orders, assigned patients and ready results.

4. How information is used

Only to run the clinic: identify patients, show the right records to the right staff, schedule visits, bill and report. Reports and printed documents are produced for the clinic's own use.

5. Who can see it

  • Access is limited by role and by clinic. Staff of one clinic cannot open another clinic's data.
  • All staff of a clinic can read its patient files. Only the doctor assigned to a patient can change that patient's clinical record, orders and invoices. Finance records are visible only to the manager and administrator.
  • The administrator can access all clinics for support and oversight.

6. Where it is stored and shared

Data is stored in a secured cloud database (Supabase) and the app is served from Vercel. These providers process data only to host it for us. A weekly backup of each clinic is created and the download links are emailed to the administrator and the clinic backup mailbox through an email service. We do not share patient information with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

7. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS encryption.
  • Sign-in with username and password; the administrator can turn on two-step verification (authenticator app).
  • Database access rules restrict data by role and clinic; changes are recorded in an activity log.
  • For offline use, a copy of the clinic's data is kept on the signed-in device and is removed when the user logs out.

No system is perfectly secure, so please keep passwords private and log out on shared devices.

8. Retention and deletion

Records are kept for as long as the clinic needs them for treatment, billing and legal obligations. A clinic can ask to correct or delete a patient record or a staff account at any time, and the administrator can delete a clinic and its data. To request deletion of your account or data, call or email us using the details below; we will act within 30 days, except where records must be kept by law.

9. Children

Children's dental records are entered by clinic staff (usually with a parent or guardian's consent). The app itself is not offered to children as users.
